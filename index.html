How it works

Four steps. One file.

  1. 1

    Nominate up to five referees.

    Former managers, peers, clients, people who reported to you. You choose. Ask while they still remember: the end of a job or a project is the best time, and the report is ready whenever you next need it.

  2. 2

    They write, in their own words.

    Each referee is confirmed by email through a private, single-use link. A few minutes each.

  3. 3

    Your report is written from what they said.

    Strengths, areas to grow and a summary, written by AI from their exact words and never made stronger than what they wrote.

  4. 4

    Add your resume. Download one file.

    Your pages first, then your report. Make it again whenever your references change.

FAQ

Questions, answered short.

What is the GoodWord report?

The GoodWord report is your credibility report: verified references from the people you worked with, in their own words. It holds validated strengths, areas to grow, a summary, whether each referee would want you on their team again and each referee's written response, shown by role. You earn it by collecting references on GoodWord.

What is Resume Pro Max?

Your resume, exactly as you wrote it, with your GoodWord report attached after it. One file, made for sending to a human. The report is what you earn. Resume Pro Max is how you send it.

Is this a background check?

No. A background check looks for problems. A GoodWord report collects what the people you worked with say about you, in their own words. Then it puts that in your hands to send.

Who can be a referee?

Former managers, peers, clients, skip-level managers and people who reported to you. You nominate up to five. You choose who.

Do my referees need an account?

No. Each referee gets a private, single-use link by email and writes their reference there. A few minutes, no sign-up.

Will recruiters see my referees' names?

By role only, such as Direct Manager or Peer, unless the referee chose to be named.

What if a referee writes something critical?

Every reference includes an area to grow, by design. That is what makes the rest believable. You cannot edit what they wrote.

What happens to my resume?

It is never stored. GoodWord reads it, merges it with your report and hands the file back in one step. Nothing stays on our servers.

Can I use it on job portals?

Use your plain resume there. Resume Pro Max is for sending to a human: a recruiter, a hiring manager, a friend who asked for your CV.

When do the individual written responses appear?

Once you have three or more references. Below that, the report shows the summary, the strengths and the verification facts.

How is this different from LinkedIn recommendations?

A LinkedIn recommendation is public. You choose it, you display it and it is never critical. A GoodWord reference is written for a private report, includes an area to grow by design, answers whether they would want you on their team again and travels inside your resume file as Resume Pro Max.

How long is a report valid?

12 months. The file itself can be remade any time your references change.

Honest by design

What keeps it honest.

  • Your resume is carried through unchanged.
  • The report is rebuilt every time you make the file.
  • Each referee is confirmed by email through a private, single-use link.
  • Anonymous by default, named only where the referee chose.
  • You cannot edit what they wrote.
  • Every reference includes an area to grow, by design.
  • Words, not numbers. No scores anywhere.
  • Your resume is never stored: read, merged, handed back, gone.
