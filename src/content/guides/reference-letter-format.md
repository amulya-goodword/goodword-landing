## The format, part by part

1. **The writer's details and the date.** Name, designation and organisation at the top, then the date. Use company letterhead only if the company allows it. A personal letter that states the writer's designation is fine.
2. **The greeting.** A name if you have one. Otherwise "To the Admissions Committee" or "To whom it may concern".
3. **The opening.** Who the writer is, how they know you and for how long.
4. **The body.** Two or three short paragraphs. One strength in each, with an example.
5. **The recommendation.** One clear sentence. "I recommend her without reservation" or "I would hire him again".
6. **The sign-off.** Signature, name, designation, phone number and an official email address.

## Rules that save trouble

- **One page.** Nobody reads the second.
- **Match your other documents.** Dates and designations must agree with your resume and your experience letter.
- **Use an official email address where possible.** Universities and employers often write back to check.
- **Send a signed PDF.** Keep the original.
- **Do not write it for them.** Many managers will say "draft it and I will sign". Give them facts, dates and examples. Let them put it in their own words. A letter that sounds like the candidate wrote it is easy to spot.

## Sample 1: from a manager, for a job

> 12 October 2026
>
> To whom it may concern,
>
> I am the Head of Operations at a logistics company in Pune. Meera Joshi reported to me as an operations executive from June 2023 to August 2026.
>
> Meera ran our returns desk, which handled about 300 cases a week. She redesigned the way cases were logged. The average time to close one fell from five days to two. She did this without extra staff.
>
> She is steady under pressure. During the monsoon disruption last year she stayed in touch with every affected customer herself. She can be reluctant to delegate, though she improved a great deal in her last year with us.
>
> I recommend Meera without reservation and would hire her again. Please contact me if you need anything further.
>
> Sincerely,
> Vikram Desai
> Head of Operations
> Phone and official email address

## Sample 2: from a professor, for higher studies

> 5 November 2026
>
> To the Admissions Committee,
>
> I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science. I taught Aditya Rao in two courses and supervised his final-year project between 2024 and 2026.
>
> Aditya was among the top five students in a class of 120 in my course on databases. For his project he built a tool to detect duplicate records in hospital data. He chose the problem himself after visiting a local clinic. He tested his work on real, messy records.
>
> He asks good questions and takes criticism well. As a member of the placement committee he coordinated visits from 14 companies, which showed me he can organise people as well as code.
>
> I recommend him strongly for your master's programme.
>
> Sincerely,
> Dr. Lakshmi Iyer
> Associate Professor, Department of Computer Science
> Phone and official email address

## Sample 3: from a client

> 20 September 2026
>
> To whom it may concern,
>
> I am the founder of a furniture business in Jaipur. Farah Khan worked with us as a freelance designer from January 2025 to July 2026.
>
> Farah redesigned our catalogue and our website. She delivered every stage on the agreed date. When we changed our minds about the photography halfway through, she replanned the work and still met the launch.
>
> She explains her choices clearly and listens when we disagree. I would work with her again and have already recommended her to two other businesses.
>
> Sincerely,
> Rohit Agarwal
> Founder
> Phone and official email address

## Is this the letter you need?

A reference letter is one person's view of your work. It is not the same as an experience letter or a relieving letter, which come from the company and state facts. If you are not sure which one you have been asked for, read [reference letter vs experience letter vs relieving letter](/guides/reference-letter-vs-experience-letter-vs-relieving-letter/).
