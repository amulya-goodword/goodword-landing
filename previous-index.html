The problem

Everyone's resume says the same thing. Yours included.

Resumes are inflated

Every applicant "led cross-functional initiatives" , "drove 40% growth". When everyone is a superman, nobody is. Recruiters know this. They discount your resume before they finish reading.

AI reads it before a human does

Your application goes through a keyword filter first. Wrong phrasing, wrong format, wrong luck - rejected. Not by a person. By a parser. You never find out why.

Effort doesn't show

Ten years of doing good work and a fresh graduate with a well-prompted resume look identical to an ATS. The system can't tell who actually delivered. So it doesn't.

There's a different way in: let the people who worked with you do the talking - verified.

How it works

Four steps. One report that speaks for you.

1

Nominate your referees

Pick up to five people who actually know your work - former managers, peers, clients. You choose who vouches for you.

2

They answer a short, verified questionnaire

Structured questions, a few minutes each. Every referee is verified - no anonymous strangers, no fabricated praise.

3

GoodWord builds your credibility report

Responses become an anonymized report: your validated strengths, your improvement areas, presented fairly. Verified end to end.

4

Share it with recruiters who can trust it

A secure link goes with your application. Recruiters open a verified reference directly - a second layer your resume can't offer.

Why it works

You can't put in a good word for yourself.

That's the whole point. A claim you make about yourself is marketing. The same claim, made by someone who worked beside you and verified by us - that's evidence.

A resume is a first-person document in a world that has stopped trusting first-person documents. GoodWord is third-party by grammar and by design. The weight comes from who is saying it and from the fact that we checked.

Verified referees

Every referee confirms their identity through a secure link before a single word counts.

Anonymized & fair

Feedback is aggregated and anonymized. Balanced by design - strengths and improvement areas, not a highlight reel.

Privacy-first

Your data is yours, per India's DPDP Act. Download it, delete it, or withdraw feedback anytime.

What recruiters told us

The people doing the hiring have noticed.

Genuine feedback from hiring managers in the GoodWord early-access pilot. Anonymized, as everything here is.

GoodWord doesn't just screen candidates. It gives the hiring manager a better interview. Recruiters are moving toward trait-and-skill hiring - judging what you can actually do, not what your resume claims. A verified credibility report is exactly the input that shift needs.

Past the AI filters

Not a hack. An Expressway.

GoodWord doesn't game the ATS or stuff keywords into your resume. It goes around the whole gauntlet: a recruiter opens a verified human reference directly. No parser in between. A candidate who puts in the effort gets seen by a person.

The keyword filter was built because recruiters couldn't trust resumes at volume. Give them something they can trust, and the filter stops being the gatekeeper.

The through-line

This rewards people who put in the work.

Traditional applying rewards keyword-stuffing and prompt-polished resumes. GoodWord rewards something else: being serious enough to ask the people you worked with to vouch for you, on the record, verified. More effort in, better results out. It's supposed to work that way.

Your waitlist data may be stored and processed on servers operated by our service providers, which may be located outside India. Where that happens, it is only with providers that are not in any country restricted for such transfers by the Government of India under the DPDP Act.

Your rights under the DPDP Act

As a Data Principal, you have the right to:

  • Access - ask us what personal data of yours we hold and how we've processed it.
  • Correction and updating - ask us to correct or complete inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure - ask us to delete your data, unless we're required to keep it by law.
  • Withdraw consent - withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawing consent means we stop processing your waitlist data and remove you from the list; it doesn't affect anything we lawfully did before you withdrew.
  • Grievance redressal - raise a complaint with us first (see below), and, if unsatisfied, escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate - nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these, write to our Grievance Officer at the contact below. We'll respond within a reasonable period as required by law.

GoodWord's waitlist is intended for working professionals and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has submitted their data, write to us and we'll delete it.

Security

We take reasonable security safeguards to protect your waitlist data against loss, unauthorised access, and disclosure. No system is perfectly secure, but the data we hold at this stage is limited by design - the less we collect, the less there is to protect.
